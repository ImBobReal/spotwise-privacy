01

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how SpotWise (“SpotWise”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the SpotWise mobile app (the “app”).

In short: SpotWise processes the content you choose to share with the app to return relevant results — and it does this without requiring an account or collecting information that identifies you.

By using SpotWise, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

02

What We Collect

We keep data collection to the minimum needed for the app to work. The list below is complete.

Content you share
When you actively share content with the app (for example, a link or an image), we process it to perform the action you requested and return your results. You decide what, if anything, to share.
Recent activity
A history of your recent activity is stored locally on your device so you can revisit it. It is not uploaded to our servers, and you can clear it any time from in-app Settings.
Anonymous usage analytics
Aggregated, non-identifying metrics about how the app performs and is used, so we can fix issues and improve it. These cannot be used to identify you.
App-integrity signals
Technical signals used to confirm that requests come from the genuine SpotWise app and to help prevent abuse. These do not identify you.
03

What We Do Not Collect

We designed SpotWise so that none of the following ever happens:

  • No account or sign-in
  • No name, email, or phone number
  • No location (precise or approximate)
  • No contacts
  • No access to your photo library
  • No payment or financial information
  • No advertising profiles or cross-app tracking
  • We never sell your data

We do not build user profiles, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Ever.

04

How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above only to:

  • provide, operate, and maintain the app and its features;
  • process the content you choose to share and return your results;
  • keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems;
  • understand overall usage so we can improve the app.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the app.

05

How Information Is Shared

We never sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

We rely on a small number of trusted service providers to operate the app. They may process data only on our behalf, and only to provide their service to us:

Cloud hosting & infrastructure
To run our backend and handle your requests.
Content processing
To process the content you choose to share and generate your results.
Analytics
To provide us with anonymous, aggregated usage and performance metrics.
App integrity & anti-abuse
To verify the app is genuine and protect the service against abuse.

Affiliate linksYour results may include links to third-party websites, and some of these may be affiliate links — meaning we may earn a commission if you make a purchase through them, at no extra cost to you. We do not share your personal information with these sites, and once you leave the app their own privacy policies apply.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or SpotWise — for example, to prevent fraud or abuse.

06

Data Retention & Deletion

We do not keep your data longer than needed:

  • Content you share is processed to produce your result and is not stored long-term. To keep the app fast, results may be cached on our servers temporarily (a few hours) and are then deleted automatically.
  • Your recent activity lives only on your device. You can clear it at any time from in-app Settings, and uninstalling the app removes all data stored locally.

To request deletion of any data associated with your use of SpotWise, email us using the details in the Contact section. We will respond within 30 days.

07

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08

Children's Privacy

SpotWise is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

09

International Data Transfers

SpotWise operates globally, and your information may be processed on servers located outside your country or region, where data-protection laws may differ. Wherever your information is processed, we protect it as described in this policy.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Because SpotWise requires no account and does not collect information that identifies you, we usually hold little or no personal data about you. To exercise any rights, or if you have questions, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date shown above. For significant changes, we will provide additional notice through the app or our store listing.

Your continued use of SpotWise after an update takes effect means you accept the revised policy.
